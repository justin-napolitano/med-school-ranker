# Mobile And Responsive Polish

## Objective

Make the existing workflow comfortable on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## In Scope

- Independent side-menu scrolling where side menus exist.
- Stable card dimensions.
- No text overflow in buttons, cards, tables, or chips.
- Tap-friendly actions.
- Sensible stacking for intake, rankings, applications, and score cards.
- School route opens at top of page.
- Collapsed groups remain easy to expand.

## Critical Checks

- Hovering/scrolling the left menu should scroll that menu independently on desktop.
- Clicking a school should not land at the bottom of the profile.
- State/filter interactions should not unexpectedly scroll the menu.
- Long school names must wrap cleanly.
- Cost and MCAT/GPA labels must fit.

## Done Criteria

- Desktop and mobile screenshots show no incoherent overlap.
- Menu and content scrolling feel controlled.
- Actions remain reachable on mobile.
- Cards and score cards stay readable.
