# Audience and Voice

## Objective

Define who the product should feel built for and how the interface should communicate.

## Primary Audience

Current and near-future medical school applicants:

- Gen Z and early Gen Alpha;
- digitally native;
- comfortable comparing multiple sources;
- skeptical of unsupported claims;
- likely to research on mobile and desktop;
- serious about application outcomes but allergic to dated institutional UX.

## Secondary Audience

- partner/collaborator reviewing school options;
- parents or advisors;
- future maintainers/admin users.

Secondary audiences should be supported without letting the visual style become parent/advisor-centered.

## Voice

Use:

- direct labels;
- clear caveats;
- concise decision language;
- transparent methodology notes;
- neutral warnings.

Avoid:

- forced slang;
- overly formal admissions-office language;
- motivational copy;
- long help text in the main UI;
- vague "best school" claims without scope.

## Done Criteria

- Applicant-facing labels are concise and trustworthy.
- Caveats are visible without sounding defensive.
- The site sounds modern without trying too hard.
